Nepal Income Tax Act, 2058 (2002) - Key Provisions for Tax Planning

Section 1 - Short Title and Commencement
This Act may be called the "Income Tax Act, 2058 (2002)". It shall come into force from the date of Chaitra 19, 2058 (April 1, 2002).

Section 2 - Definitions
"Person" includes natural person, entity, and couple.
"Income year" means the period of 12 months commencing from the 1st of Shrawan.
"Assessable income" means the total income minus exempt amounts.

Section 5 - Imposition of Income Tax
Income tax shall be imposed on the assessable income of each person for each income year.

Section 10 - Tax Rates for Natural Persons
For individuals with employment income:
- First NPR 500,000: 1% social security tax
- Next NPR 200,000: 10%
- Next NPR 300,000: 20%
- Next NPR 1,000,000: 30%
- Remaining: 36%

Section 11 - Couple Filing
A couple may elect to file jointly. When filing jointly, the tax-free threshold is increased by 50%.

Section 12 - Deductions from Employment Income
(1) Contributions to approved retirement funds are deductible up to NPR 300,000 per year.
(2) Life insurance premiums are deductible up to NPR 40,000 per year.
(3) Medical insurance premiums paid by the employer are not taxable to the employee.

Section 16 - Business Income
Business income includes profits and gains from any business conducted by a person.

Section 17 - Deductions Allowed from Business Income
(1) General deductions: expenses incurred in gaining or producing the assessable income.
(2) Interest: interest on money borrowed and used in the business is deductible.
(3) Depreciation: as per Schedule 2.
(4) Research and development expenses are deductible.
(5) Repair and improvement expenses up to 7% of depreciation base.

Section 18 - Non-Deductible Expenses
(1) Personal or domestic expenses
(2) Tax imposed under this Act
(3) Fines, penalties, and similar payments
(4) Expenses of a capital nature (except as allowed under depreciation)

Section 23 - Tax Credits
(1) Foreign tax credit: tax paid in a foreign country on foreign-source income can be credited.
(2) Medical tax credit for disabled persons.

Section 47 - Withholding Tax (TDS)
Various rates for different payment types:
- Employment income: as per Section 10 rates
- Rent: 10% of gross amount
- Service fees: 15%
- Dividends: 5%

Section 50 - Advance Tax
Persons with estimated tax liability exceeding NPR 5,000 must pay advance tax in three installments.

Section 56 - Penalties
Failure to file returns: NPR 100 per month or 0.1% of assessable income.
Understatement of income: 50% of underpaid tax.

Schedule 1 - Tax Rates for Entities
Entities other than natural persons:
- Banks, financial institutions, insurance: 30%
- Telecom, cigarette, liquor: 30%
- Special industries: 20%
- Other entities: 25%
- Cooperatives: Special rates (see Cooperative Tax provisions)

Schedule 2 - Depreciation Rates
Category A (Buildings): 5% declining balance
Category B (Furniture, fixtures): 25% declining balance
Category C (Vehicles): 20% declining balance
Category D (Machinery): 15% declining balance
Category E (Computers): 25% declining balance

Note: Section 24 provides for investment allowances for special industries.
Section 24A: Industries established in remote areas get additional deductions.
Section 24B: Export-oriented industries get reduced tax rates.
